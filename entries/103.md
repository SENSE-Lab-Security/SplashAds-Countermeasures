### What it does

Uses Xposed/LSPosed to press app hook advertising SDK, normal UI or Flutter channel and other different entrances to block or automatically skip after hitting the advertising logic.

### Limits and notes

It is not a unified solution and relies on app-by-app adaptation; Root/LSPosed is required. The specific support scope and behavior should be further verified before use.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability; please only grant accessibility permissions to trusted sources

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Positive energy is injected into multiple adapted Apps, and different call points such as advertising SDK, UI methods or Flutter MethodChannel are monitored according to the implementation of each app.<br>
**How it decides:** Based on the known advertising call points configured/adapted on an app-by-app basis, rather than a unified universal identification.<br>
**What it does:** hook at the advertising SDK/app logic/UI channel and modify the execution results; in some scenarios, cooperate with automatic taps.<br>
**Possible result:** Some ads in adapted apps are blocked or automatically skipped, but the coverage depends on the specific version and rules.

### Coverage
