### What it does

Detect visible Skip buttons and automatically click them through accessibility services.

### Limits and notes

Mainly covers ads with identifiable Skip/Close controls; the specific identification rules and data processing methods of the closed source/store version are not easy to audit.

### Before use

Only grant accessibility permissions to trusted sources

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** AdSkipper Depends on Accessibility Services Observe Accessible UI,<br>
**How it decides:** Focus on looking for the Skip/Close control that has appeared.<br>
**What it does:** Directly call barrier-free click after matching the corresponding node;<br>
**Possible result:** It solves the problem of "helping users click out ads", rather than blocking ad network requests or triggering logic in advance.

### Coverage

Display-only｜Skip/Close controls that have been displayed and can be read by Accessibility

### Intervention point

Accessibility services + UI nodes/keywords/coordinate rules + click gestures
