### What it does

Uses accessibility services to sense startup pages and accessible controls, and automatically click on skip buttons after locating them based on local/subscription rules.

### Limits and notes

It does not prevent ad loading or triggering by shaking; rules may become invalid as the app UI changes, and self-drawn controls may not be recognized by the accessibility tree.

### Before use

Only grant accessibility permissions to trusted sources

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** SKIP uses Accessibility Services to listen for app startup, window changes, and read the current page's accessible controls.<br>
**How it decides:** Matches the text, control properties, etc. in a specific app/page with the "skip/close" target based on local/subscription rules.<br>
**What it does:** Calls Accessibility's click ability to operate the matching control during the opening ad UI phase.<br>
**Possible result:** The ad itself may have been displayed, but the skip action was completed automatically; network loading and shake triggering themselves were not blocked by the source.

### Coverage

Display-only｜Accessible skip button in normal startup page/splash ad, especially the page where subscription rules have been adapted

### Intervention point

Accessibility Services + Rules/Subscription Matching
