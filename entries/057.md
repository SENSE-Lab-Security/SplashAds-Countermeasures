### What it does

Uses network capabilities such as DNSCrypt and firewall to take over DNS/traffic and block unwanted advertising or tracking connections according to rules.

### Limits and notes

It is not a dedicated splash advertising tool, and the configuration is complicated; advertising and normal content sharing the same domain, caching ads, etc. still cannot be solved.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability; may take over some network connections or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** InviZible Pro touches the device's DNS/network traffic via DNSCrypt, firewall/VPN, or Root network paths.<br>
**How it decides:** Determines whether the connection should be filtered based on configured domain/network rules, rather than identifying advertising UI.<br>
**What it does:** Deny hit domains/connections at the DNS or network connection stage.<br>
**Possible result:** Related advertising/tracking resources cannot be loaded normally through the Internet.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜Advertising, tracking and privacy-related connections hit by DNSCrypt/firewall rules

### Intervention point

DNSCrypt + Firewall/VPN/Root Network Control
