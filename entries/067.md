### What it does

Uses a local firewall to block known trackers, ads, and malicious domains in all apps.

### Limits and notes

Based on domain list, cannot handle first-party or built-in ads; may conflict with other VPN configurations.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** After the user configures filtering, applied DNS or network requests enter the tool.<br>
**How it decides:** When deciding, compare domain, IP, URL or request characteristics to ad/tracking rules.<br>
**What it does:** After a hit, deny the target application/endpoint during the connection establishment or DNS phase.<br>
**Possible result:** As a result, the hit ad/tracking resource cannot be loaded from the network; first-party shared domains, caches, and built-in content may still be bypassed.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional) | Known trackers, ads and malicious domains hit by the iOS local firewall list

### Intervention point

DNS/network request filtering + advertising domain or connection rules
