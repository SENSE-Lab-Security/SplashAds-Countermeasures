### What it does

Leverage native VPN, DNS, or platform content filtering capabilities to match ad/tracking requests and block connections before the resource is loaded.

### Limits and notes

Closed source; it cannot be inferred from product promotion alone that it will be effective for Shake or all native app ads.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Leverage local VPN, DNS or platform content filtering capabilities to match ad/tracking requests and block the connection before the resource is loaded.<br>
**How it decides:** When enabled, relevant DNS/network requests will first go through its filtering path;<br>
**What it does:** The tool determines whether to pass based on the domain, target address, app rules or filtering rules maintained by the project. After a hit, it is directly rejected before resolution or connection establishment.<br>
**Possible result:** This allows the corresponding ad resources to fail before downloading, but it does not determine the advertising content on the screen or whether the user has the intention to jump.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜Advertising and tracking requests hit by its local VPN/DNS/content filtering rules

### Intervention point

Local VPN (VpnService) + DNS/Connection Filtering
