### What it does

Uses a local DNS proxy/VPN to receive queries and use filter lists to block advertising/tracking domains during the name resolution phase.

### Limits and notes

Only affects online ads that require DNS resolution; cached content or ads in the same domain as normal content are difficult to filter individually.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** personalDNSfilter acts as a local DNS proxy (can be used with VPN on Android) to receive DNS queries from the app.<br>
**How it decides:** Determines whether the requested domain belongs to the advertising/tracking target that needs to be blocked based on the filtering list.<br>
**What it does:** Reject or overwrite the resolution results of hit domains at the DNS stage.<br>
**Possible result:** Ad servers that rely on these domains cannot connect properly.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜Advertising/tracking domains hit in the filter list in the local DNS proxy

### Intervention point

Local DNS Proxy/VpnService + Domain Name Filter List
