### What it does

Uses the advertising methods or data flows inside Xposed/LSPosed/LSPatch hook NGA to filter or skip advertising logic before the splash screen/information flow content is displayed.

### Limits and notes

Only works on specific apps; may break after the target app is updated. Using Xposed/LSPosed usually has a high deployment threshold.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability; modified applications may bring signature, update, account and supply chain risks

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** PureNGA injects hook code into the NGA process and intercepts pre-adapted ad-related methods/data processing paths.<br>
**How it decides:** Based on known ad call points or ad data structures within a specific version of NGA, rather than generic ad identification.<br>
**What it does:** Filters results, modify return values, or skip calls directly before the app’s internal advertising logic/data enters the UI.<br>
**Possible result:** The corresponding open screen/information flow ad will not enter the display process normally.

### Coverage

Both (advertising logic layer)｜NGA’s splash advertising and information flow advertising
