### What it does

For Quzhi Campus 6.5.28, remove splash screen and page ads and block shake to jump.

### Limits and notes

Only specific Quzhi Campus versions are supported; relocation is required after the resource ID, advertising SDK or call chain changes.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** QzxyAdBlock is injected into Quzhi Campus 6.5.28 through LSPosed, and monitors the screen opening, page advertising and shake-related codes that have been targeted by the project.<br>
**How it decides:** It relies on known advertising methods, data or shake paths in this specific version and does not do general advertising identification.<br>
**What it does:** Returns early at the corresponding call point, remove ad data, or truncate the shake processing chain.<br>
**Possible result:** Ad display and some shake-triggered jumps in this version will be invalid at the same time; they need to be re-adapted after the version changes.

### Coverage

Both｜Quzhi Campus 6.5.28’s splash screen/page ad and shake-to-jump logic

### Intervention point

Root; LSPosed + app/SDK/UI runtime hook
