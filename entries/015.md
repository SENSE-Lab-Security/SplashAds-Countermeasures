### What it does

Uses Xposed to hook shake/sensor-related registration or callback logic in the target app, preventing shake signals from continuing into the ad redirect logic.

### Limits and notes

It mainly blocks shake/motion triggers and does not directly affect other paths such as taps, swipes, or automatic redirects; the hook may also stop working if the app changes its internal shake implementation.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Fuck Shake injects the user-selected app via Xposed and hooks registration or callback logic related to accelerometer/shake detection.<br>
**How it decides:** Instead of identifying ad pages, it treats hit shake/sensor related calls as trigger chain entries that need to be suppressed.<br>
**What it does:** Prevents related registration, callback or subsequent processing from continuing to execute at the sensor/shake callback layer.<br>
**Possible result:** Shaking the phone can no longer drive the shake-triggered ad redirect logic in the app; other triggers such as taps and swipes may still exist.

### Coverage

Navigation-triggering｜Ad redirect chain triggered by accelerometer/shake; does not cover taps, swipes and other triggers

### Intervention point

Xposed + Shake/Sensor Trigger hook
