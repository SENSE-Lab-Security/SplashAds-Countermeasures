### What it does

Removes ads, sponsored apps, and promoted recommendations from the Google Play Store.

### Limits and notes

Only the sponsored/promoted content within Google Play will be processed and will not affect the opening ads of other apps.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** The corresponding platform, permissions or runtime environment need to be configured according to the original project instructions.<br>
**How it decides:** Intercepts related data or UI logic when generating or assembling sponsored/promoted items in the store.<br>
**What it does:** Filters or remove the corresponding promotion item after hitting it,<br>
**Possible result:** So it's targeting Google Play's own feed/recommended ads, not generic splash ads.

### Coverage

Display-only | Sponsored apps, promotions, and ad cards in the Google Play Store
