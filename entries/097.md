### What it does

Uses LSPosed to inject specific apps and hook known advertising methods/data paths to modify or skip logic before screen opening, banner or video advertising is executed.

### Limits and notes

Highly dependent on the version and internal implementation of the target app; hook points may change after the app is updated and need to be re-adapted. Requires Root/LSPosed.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** FanqieHook injects the supported target app through LSPosed and waits for the pre-targeted ad-related methods to be called.<br>
**How it decides:** Based on known advertising classes, methods, or data flows in a specific app version, rather than dynamically identifying screen content.<br>
**What it does:** Blocks execution at the app/advertising logic layer by returning early, modifying parameters/return values, or filtering ad data.<br>
**Possible result:** Corresponding to screen opening, banner, video and other ads being skipped or invalidated before entering the complete display process.

### Coverage

Both (advertising logic layer)｜Open screen ads, banners, and video ads for specific apps

### Intervention point

LSPosed + app-specific advertising logic hook
