### What it does

Blocks splash screens, banners, information flows, interstitials, native and rewarded advertising interfaces for super curriculum.

### Limits and notes

Only for super curriculum and identified advertising interfaces; the adaptation may become invalid after the target version changes.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** After XTA-AdKiller is injected into the super curriculum,<br>
**How it decides:** Blocks the splash screen, banner, information flow, interstitial, native and rewarded advertising interfaces that the project has targeted.<br>
**What it does:** When the corresponding interface is called, directly rewrite and return or skip the advertising process,<br>
**Possible result:** So it's dealing with the app's internal advertising interface, not the on-screen "skip" button.

### Coverage

Display-only | Open screen, banner, information flow, interstitial, native and rewarded advertising interfaces in the super curriculum

### Intervention point

Root; LSPosed + app/SDK/UI runtime hook
