### What it does

Removes sponsored cards, promotional pins, ad suggestions, and reservation ads after Google Maps parses the response.

### Limits and notes

Only for adapted sponsored content in Google Maps; not network-level or splash ad defense.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Maps AdBlock steps in after Google Maps receives and parses the response,<br>
**How it decides:** Identifies sponsorship cards, promotion pins, ad suggestions and reservation ad fields that the project has adapted.<br>
**What it does:** It filters out the corresponding data before the content enters the map UI,<br>
**Possible result:** So ads are not coming and going by web domain or screen taps.

### Coverage

Display-only | Sponsored cards, promotional pins, ad suggestions and booking ads in Google Maps responses

### Intervention point

Root; LSPosed + app/SDK/UI runtime hook
