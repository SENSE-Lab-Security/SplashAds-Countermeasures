### What it does

Used to handle ad requests, ad interfaces, or ad redirects, depending on the apps and versions supported by the project.

### Limits and notes

It is a development library rather than an independent system-level blocker and needs to be integrated into the WebView of the target app/browser; it cannot directly handle native Android View ads, sensor triggers or cross-app jumps.

### Before use

Check the source, version, and permissions before use.

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Developers integrate AdblockAndroid into the WebView of the target app/browser, and the filtering engine can see the resource loading and page content of the WebView.<br>
**How it decides:** Identifies ad requests or ad page elements based on EasyList, AdGuard Filters, custom rules, CSS selector and scriptlet rules.<br>
**What it does:** Blocks matching requests during the WebView resource loading phase and perform element hiding, CSS/Extended CSS or scriptlets during the page rendering phase.<br>
**Possible result:** The ad resources in WebView can not be loaded, and the advertising DOM/elements that have entered the page can also be hidden or scripted.

### Coverage

Display-only (WebView content layer)｜Ad network requests, HTML/DOM advertising elements, styles and scripts in WebView
