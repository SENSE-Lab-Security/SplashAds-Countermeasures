### What it does

Removes the Sponsored insert from the Threads stream.

### Limits and notes

Only handles Sponsored inserts in the Threads information flow, and does not cover splash ads or other apps.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Threads Hide Ads inject official Threads,<br>
**How it decides:** Identifies project-adapted Sponsored inserts when assembling data in a feed,<br>
**What it does:** and filter them out before they make it to the list.<br>
**Possible result:** It does not analyze the screen image, but directly processes the information flow data/components inside Threads.

### Coverage

Display-only | Sponsored inserts in Threads streams
