### What it does

Hide ads, sponsored cards, video carousels, and ad slots for the Amazon Shopping app.

### Limits and notes

Only for the adapted advertising/recommendation component of Amazon Shopping, the internal data or view structure may change after the application is revised.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** amznkiller injects Amazon Shopping through LSPosed/LSPatch and observes the adapted data/View path during the generation of information flow and page components.<br>
**How it decides:** Determines what content needs to be removed based on the project's known Sponsored card, ad slot, and video carousel data/component characteristics.<br>
**What it does:** Filters the data or hide the corresponding View before these items enter the final interface.<br>
**Possible result:** Sponsored cards, video carousels and ad slots are removed from the page, while normal product content is retained as much as possible.

### Coverage

Display-only | Sponsored cards, video carousels, and ad slots in Amazon Shopping
