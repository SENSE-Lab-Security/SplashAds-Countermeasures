### What it does

Uses Xposed/LSPosed hook to open screen Activity, Splash container and third-party advertising SDK initialization, and block advertising components before creation/SDK startup.

### Limits and notes

Rely on internal classes and methods of the app/SDK; code obfuscation, version updates, dynamic loading or native implementation will reduce hook stability.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** LiTianSuo injects the target app and monitors adapted calls such as screen opening activity, Splash container creation, and third-party advertising SDK initialization.<br>
**How it decides:** The ad process is being initiated based on the known classes, components, and methods of a specific app/SDK.<br>
**What it does:** hook directly, return early or skip component startup during Activity/container creation or advertising SDK initialization phase.<br>
**Possible result:** The advertising component or SDK cannot be fully started, and open/full screen ads may be blocked before entering the UI.

### Coverage

Display-only｜Full-screen/splash ads driven by open-screen Activity, Splash container or third-party ad SDK initialization
