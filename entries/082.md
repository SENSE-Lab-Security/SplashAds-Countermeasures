### What it does

IVPN's DNS-level AntiTracker blocks ads and tracking domains within VPN tunnels.

### Limits and notes

Relying on the domain list, it is not possible to identify advertising UIs, sensor triggers, or ads that are co-domained with normal content.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** IVPN AntiTracker filters domains on the VPN/DNS server.<br>
**How it decides:** After a device's request enters its tunnel or resolver, it is compared against a list of ads, tracking or malicious domains,<br>
**What it does:** The hit resolution/connection was refused, so the corresponding network resource cannot be loaded;<br>
**Possible result:** If the ad is in the same domain as normal content, has been cached, or is directly built into the app, it will not pass this judgment.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜Advertising and tracking domains hit by IVPN AntiTracker list

### Intervention point

VPN tunnel + server-side DNS/domain filtering
