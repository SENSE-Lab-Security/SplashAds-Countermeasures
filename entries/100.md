### What it does

Uses the Xposed/LSPosed hook advertising function/data flow within Weibo to modify or filter the results before the splash screen or information flow ad is displayed.

### Limits and notes

Depends on target app version; requires Root/LSPosed. hook rules may need to be maintained after app updates or ad implementation changes.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** ReWeibo injects Weibo-related processes and intercepts pre-adapted advertising functions or data processing processes.<br>
**How it decides:** Relies on known ad call points/data structures in a specific Weibo version and does not perform general visual ad recognition.<br>
**What it does:** Modifies the return value, filter ad data or bypass related methods in the app advertising logic/data layer.<br>
**Possible result:** The corresponding open screen/information flow ad cannot be displayed normally.

### Coverage

Both (advertising logic layer)｜Weibo screen advertising, some information flow advertising
