### What it does

Clicks are triggered based on the presence or absence of an on-screen image and can be configured to recognize and click the ad skip button.

### Limits and notes

It is a general automation tool and does not come with advertising semantics; whether it can stably skip ads depends on the image conditions and page changes configured by the user.

### Before use

Only grant accessibility permissions to trusted sources

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Klick'r is more like a universal visual auto-clicker: it takes the screen,<br>
**How it decides:** Uses user-configured image conditions to determine whether a button or area appears,<br>
**What it does:** Click through accessibility gestures after the conditions are met.<br>
**Possible result:** The "skip" button can be used for advertising after being made into a recognition condition, but the tool itself has no advertising semantics.

### Coverage

Display-only | User-defined button/area with screen image conditions; can be used for visually stable ad skip buttons

### Intervention point

Accessibility Services + Screen Image Matching + gesture Clicking
