### What it does

Implement version-specific Hooks for shake-to-open ads for Cainiao, Bilibili and QQ.

### Limits and notes

Only the adapted Cainiao, Bilibili, QQ and corresponding versions are covered; the relevant hook points may become invalid after the target app is updated.

### Before use

It may affect system security or application stability; it is more suitable for developers or researchers and cannot be installed directly as a normal app.

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** CleanSplash injects adapted apps such as Cainiao, Bilibili, QQ, etc. through<br>
**How it decides:** It relies on known advertising methods, sensor registrations or callback paths in a specific app/version to determine that the relevant process is being executed, rather than recognizing the screen image.<br>
**What it does:** Directly overwrite the return value after a hit, skip the callback, or prevent subsequent calls from continuing to execute.<br>
**Possible result:** The splash ad or shake-triggered jump chain in the corresponding version is cut off; the hook point may need to be re-adapted after the app is updated.
