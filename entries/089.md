### What it does

Uses AccessibilityService to listen to UI events and identify target nodes according to preset rules, and then automatically skip ads through accessible taps.

### Limits and notes

More of a tutorial/prototype than a mature product; relies on rules and accessible UI, with limited stability and maintainability.

### Before use

Please only grant accessibility permissions to trusted sources; more suitable for developers or researchers, and cannot be installed directly as a normal app

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** AdSkipHelper listens to interface events through AccessibilityService and reads the current UI.<br>
**How it decides:** Matches the page/node with the preset rules. When hit, it is considered that there is an ad or skip control that needs to be processed.<br>
**What it does:** Calls accessibility click capabilities to perform actions during the UI phase.<br>
**Possible result:** Open-screen ads that meet the rules can be automatically skipped; this project is more of a prototype/teaching implementation.

### Coverage

Display-only｜Open screen advertising pages or skip nodes hit by default rules

### Intervention point

Accessibility Services + UI Event/Rule Matching
