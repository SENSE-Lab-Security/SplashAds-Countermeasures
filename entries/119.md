### What it does

Used to handle ad requests, ad interfaces, or ad redirects, depending on the apps and versions supported by the project.

### Limits and notes

Morphe Manager itself is not dedicated to advertising, and the ability to remove ads is provided by Adobo Patches; the scope of support depends on the patch and app/SDK version. Static modifications may affect signing, updates, and compatibility.

### Before use

Modified applications may bring signature, update, account and supply chain risks

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** The user selects the target APK in Morphe Manager and imports/enables Adobo's anti-ad patches.<br>
**How it decides:** Adobo matches advertising logic based on specific apps, advertising SDKs, bytecode or component characteristics; its public patches include Reddit/9GAG/IMDb removal of advertising and general mobile advertising SDK disabling.<br>
**What it does:** Follow the project instructions for processing at the request, interface or jump stage.<br>
**Possible result:** In the rebuilt app, the hit advertising/telemetry logic cannot run or be displayed according to the original process.
