### What it does

The ad removal and image quality unlocking module for Bilibili 7.4.0 can remove normal screen opening and multi-page ads.

### Limits and notes

Strictly relies on the internal implementation of specific old versions of Bilibili and is not a universal splash advertising solution.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** bili-hook is injected into the Bilibili process through LSPosed and waits for the adapted internal functions related to splash ads to be called.<br>
**How it decides:** Based on the known methods/call points in a specific version of Bilibili, this is the splash ad process.<br>
**What it does:** Advertise the logic layer hook method inside the app and modify the return value, return early or bypass the call.<br>
**Possible result:** The splash ad loading/display process was skipped or could not be completed normally.

### Coverage

Display-only｜Normal open screen and adapted page ads in Bilibili 7.4.0

### Intervention point

Root; LSPosed + app/SDK/UI runtime hook
