### What it does

Windscribe account-side DNS filtering system blocks ads, trackers, and custom categories.

### Limits and notes

It relies on server-side domain classification, cannot handle same-domain, cached or built-in ads, and does not understand trigger/jump semantics within the app.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Windscribe R.O.B.E.R.T. filters domains on the VPN/DNS server.<br>
**How it decides:** After a device's request enters its tunnel or resolver, it is compared against a list of ads, tracking or malicious domains,<br>
**What it does:** The hit resolution/connection was refused, so the corresponding network resource cannot be loaded;<br>
**Possible result:** If the ad is in the same domain as normal content, has been cached, or is directly built into the app, it will not pass this judgment.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜Advertising, tracking and custom category domains hit by R.O.B.E.R.T. account rules

### Intervention point

VPN tunnel + server-side DNS/domain filtering
