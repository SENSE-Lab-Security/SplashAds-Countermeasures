### What it does

Uses a local VPN or root proxy/iptables to take over traffic and block matching connections based on advertised domain rules.

### Limits and notes

Relying on domain/network characteristics can be distinguished individually; cached, built-in or first-party ads may still be bypassed.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability; may take over some network connections or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Takes over DNS/network via local VPN without Root; Root mode can intervene in traffic via proxy or iptables.<br>
**How it decides:** Determines whether the request needs to be blocked based on the ad domain/network rules, without analyzing the page content.<br>
**What it does:** Reject matching ad requests during the DNS/connection phase.<br>
**Possible result:** The ad resources hit by the rule cannot be loaded from the network normally.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜app ad requests whose ad domains/network rules hit

### Intervention point

Local VPN/Root proxy or iptables + domain rules
