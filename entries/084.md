### What it does

DNS-level blocking of ads, trackers, and malicious domains provided by PIA VPN.

### Limits and notes

Only domain-level blocking is performed, and first-party or ads in the same domain as normal content may still appear; the available capabilities of different platforms may be inconsistent.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** The corresponding platform, permissions or runtime environment need to be configured according to the original project instructions.<br>
**How it decides:** After a device's request enters its tunnel or resolver, it is compared against a list of ads, tracking or malicious domains,<br>
**What it does:** The hit resolution/connection was refused, so the corresponding network resource cannot be loaded;<br>
**Possible result:** If the ad is in the same domain as normal content, has been cached, or is directly built into the app, it will not pass this judgment.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜Advertising, tracking or malicious domains hit by PIA VPN DNS list

### Intervention point

VPN tunnel + server-side DNS/domain filtering
