### What it does

Uses local or cloud DNS/VPN paths to identify advertising and tracking domains and block hit requests before network resources are loaded.

### Limits and notes

The filtering architecture differs between versions; first-party, shared domain, and cached ads may still appear.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** The device's DNS or network requests enter Blokada's filtering path; the specific network architecture varies between versions.<br>
**How it decides:** Determines whether the target request needs to be intercepted based on the advertising/tracking domain list, and does not directly identify the opening advertising page.<br>
**What it does:** Blocks hit requests during the DNS resolution or network connection phase.<br>
**Possible result:** Ads that rely on these network resources may not load properly; cached/first-party ads may still appear.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional) | advertising and tracking domains/requests hit by their DNS/network filtering lists

### Intervention point

Local VPN (VpnService) + DNS/Connection Filtering
