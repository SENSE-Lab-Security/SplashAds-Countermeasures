### What it does

Uses libxposed/LSPosed hook to modify the execution results during the loading/display phase of the image/video splash ad and promotion code in Xiaomi Sports Health.

### Limits and notes

Only applicable to the adapted version of Xiaomi Sports Health; the hook point needs to be repositioned after the internal advertising method of the app changes.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** MiFitnessAdAway injects the Xiaomi sports health process through libxposed/LSPosed and intercepts the adapted splash advertising/promotion related code paths.<br>
**How it decides:** Determines the flow based on known advertising methods or call points in a specific version of the app.<br>
**What it does:** hook the target method during the ad loading/display logic phase and modify the return result or skip the call.<br>
**Possible result:** Image/video opening ads and some promotional content cannot be displayed normally according to the original process.

### Coverage

Display-only｜Picture/video splash ads and some promotional content in Xiaomi Sports Health
