### What it does

Uses the View creation/layout process of Xposed hook app to identify and remove advertising views while reclaiming the page space originally occupied.

### Limits and notes

Not specific to splash ads; the project is archived and modern custom-rendered UIs, WebViews, or new ad frameworks may not be recognized by the old rules.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** MinMinGuard injects the app through Xposed and intercepts View creation/layout related processes.<br>
**How it decides:** Determines whether a View should be considered an ad based on its Ad View identification rules; not based on the screen time window or shake behavior.<br>
**What it does:** Hide/remove the hit ad view in the UI View layer and adjust the layout.<br>
**Possible result:** The corresponding in-app ad controls and blank areas are removed; modern self-drawing/WebView and other implementations may not be recognized similarly.

### Coverage

Display-only｜In-app ad controls and their placeholders recognized by the traditional Android View layer

### Intervention point

Xposed + View creation/layout hook
