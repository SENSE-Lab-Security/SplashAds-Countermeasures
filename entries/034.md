### What it does

Filters promoted content in X/Twitter's timeline, details, search, and video tabs.

### Limits and notes

Only handles promoted content that has been adapted within X/Twitter; needs to be updated when the timeline structure or obfuscation code changes.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Twitter Hide Ads are injected into X/Twitter to observe data entries in content generation paths such as timeline, details, search, and video tags.<br>
**How it decides:** Determines whether an item is promoted content based on the promoted tag/data structure that the project has adapted.<br>
**What it does:** Filters the corresponding data/component before promoted items enter the final list or UI.<br>
**Possible result:** The promoted content in the adapted page is removed, but this does not handle launch ads or ads from other apps.

### Coverage

Display-only｜X/Promoted content in Twitter timeline, details, search, and video tags
