### What it does

NordVPN’s threat protection features include ad and malicious domain blocking, with specific capabilities varying by platform.

### Limits and notes

The functional levels of different platforms are not consistent, and the content scanning capabilities of the desktop cannot be directly extrapolated to the mobile terminal; the mobile terminal is still mainly limited by the domain/network layer granularity.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** NordVPN Threat Protection filters domains on the VPN/DNS server.<br>
**How it decides:** After a device's request enters its tunnel or resolver, it is compared against a list of ads, tracking or malicious domains,<br>
**What it does:** The hit resolution/connection was refused, so the corresponding network resource cannot be loaded;<br>
**Possible result:** If the ad is in the same domain as normal content, has been cached, or is directly built into the app, it will not pass this judgment.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜Mobile Threat Protection can identify ads and malicious domain requests

### Intervention point

VPN tunnel + server-side DNS/domain filtering
