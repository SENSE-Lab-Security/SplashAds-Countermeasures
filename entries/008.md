### What it does

Uses Accessibility Services to sense app startup and automatically click on skip/close controls based on local rules within the short startup scan window.

### Limits and notes

Without blocking ad network requests, the ad may still be displayed first; if the skip button is not in the accessibility tree or appears outside the scanning window, it may be missed.

### Before use

Only grant accessibility permissions to trusted sources

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** SplashCleaner<br>
**How it decides:** Only scan the current page during the short startup window.<br>
**What it does:** It looks for accessibility controls such as "Skip/Close" based on local rules, and taps immediately when found,<br>
**Possible result:** So no long-term staring at all pages, no blocking of ads from the network layer.

### Coverage

Display-only｜Accessible "skip/close" control that appears within a short window after the app is launched

### Intervention point

Accessibility Services + Local Rules + Start Scan Window
