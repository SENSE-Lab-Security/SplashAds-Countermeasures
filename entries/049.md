### What it does

Uses Android VpnService to take over each app network connection, and use app firewall and hosts rules to block specified connections or ad domains.

### Limits and notes

It is mainly a general firewall that does not understand the semantics of splash ads; shared domains and cached ads may still pass, and local VPN mode will occupy VPN slots.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** NetGuard sees the network connections initiated by each app through VpnService.<br>
**How it decides:** First determine whether to allow it based on the app networking rules configured by the user; enable hosts filtering and then use the domain list to identify advertising requests.<br>
**What it does:** Deny traffic from specified apps or hit advertising domains during the connection establishment/DNS phase.<br>
**Possible result:** The corresponding network request cannot be completed, and the network-loaded ad or tracking resource cannot be obtained.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜The network connection that is rejected according to the app firewall rules, and the ad domain hit in the hosts list

### Intervention point

Local VPN (VpnService) + Filter by app Firewall + hosts
