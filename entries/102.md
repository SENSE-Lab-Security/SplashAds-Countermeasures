### What it does

Leverage Xposed/LSPosed hook key calls for app-specific splash ads to return or modify results early at runtime to bypass display.

### Limits and notes

The project scale is small, and the specific supported apps/versions need to be further verified; Root/LSPosed is required and may become invalid after updating.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** TheMessWorld injects the target app and waits for known key methods of opening the screen ad to be called.<br>
**How it decides:** Relying on the project's pre-adaptation for a specific app/version, hitting the specified method is considered an advertising process.<br>
**What it does:** Returns or modify method results in advance through Xposed/LSPosed at the app logic layer.<br>
**Possible result:** The corresponding splash advertising process is bypassed; unadapted versions/implementations may not be effective.
