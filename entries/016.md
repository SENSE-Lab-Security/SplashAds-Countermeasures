### What it does

Uses the Accessibility Service to determine that the target app has just started, and then call the restricted mode/whitelist mechanism of SensorService through Shizuku to temporarily block the motion sensor during the splash window.

### Limits and notes

Mainly for motion sensor triggering in the startup phase, tap/swipe ads will still exist; when the time window or SensorService control range is inappropriate, normal sensor functions may also be temporarily affected.

### Before use

Only grant accessibility permissions to trusted sources

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** NoShakeAD uses Accessibility Services to sense foreground app switching and confirm that the user has just opened the configured target app.<br>
**How it decides:** It does not identify ad screens, but uses "a few seconds after the target app is launched" as a high-risk splash window.<br>
**What it does:** Uses Shizuku to call the restricted mode/whitelist mechanism of SensorService, so that the target app cannot receive normal motion sensor events during this period, and then recover.<br>
**Possible result:** Open-screen ads may still be displayed, but the shake trigger chain that relies on acceleration/motion data will fail at the input layer.

### Coverage

Navigation-triggering｜Shake-to-open ads that rely on motion sensors within seconds after the app is launched
