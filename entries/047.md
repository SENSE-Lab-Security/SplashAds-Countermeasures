### What it does

Uses system hosts or Android local VPN to take over DNS resolution, and use advertising domain blocklist to block matching requests.

### Limits and notes

Depends on ad domains being identifiable separately; coverage is limited when ads share a domain with normal content, are already cached, or are bundled directly into the app.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability; may take over some network connections or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** In Root mode, system hosts participate in DNS resolution; in Root-less mode, Android VpnService takes over DNS/network requests.<br>
**How it decides:** Compares the domain requested by the app with the ad domain in hosts/blocklist, and does not determine whether the current UI is an ad.<br>
**What it does:** In the DNS/connection stage, resolve the hit ad domain to an invalid address or directly refuse the connection.<br>
**Possible result:** Advertising resources cannot be loaded normally from the corresponding server; ads that have been cached or are in the same domain as normal content are not directly affected by this mechanism.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜Ads and tracking domains that hit hosts/blocklist; can cover some network-loaded splash ads

### Intervention point

hosts file / local VPN (VpnService) + domain blocklist
