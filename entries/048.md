### What it does

Provides DNS blacklisting, local VPN, per-app firewall, traffic logs, and optional WireGuard.

### Limits and notes

Ads that share domains with your business cannot be reliably distinguished; local VPN mode also competes with other VPNs for the same system slot.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Rethink establishes a local VPN (VpnService) so that app traffic and DNS queries go through its network stack.<br>
**How it decides:** Allow traffic based on DNS blocklist, target domain/connection, and user-configured per-app firewall rules, rather than identifying ad UI.<br>
**What it does:** Reject hit requests directly during the network connection establishment or DNS resolution phase.<br>
**Possible result:** The advertising/tracking resource hit by the rule cannot be loaded online.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜Advertising/tracking domains hit by DNS blocklist, and connections rejected by app firewall rules

### Intervention point

Local VPN (VpnService) + DNS/Connection Filtering
