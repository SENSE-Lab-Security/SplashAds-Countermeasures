### What it does

LSPosed module for "auto-clicker" apps that skips their opening ads and removes their banners.

### Limits and notes

Only for specific automatic clicker apps, and the project also contains non-advertising functions, all behaviors cannot be classified as ad protection.

### Before use

It may affect system security or application stability; it is more suitable for developers or researchers and cannot be installed directly as a normal app.

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** AutoClickerPurifier directly injects the target "automatic clicker" app,<br>
**How it decides:** Make Hooks for its known splash ad and banner ad codes.<br>
**What it does:** Skip calls or remove content when these methods or View creation logic are hit,<br>
**Possible result:** So ads are handled in app internal logic/UI generation phase.
