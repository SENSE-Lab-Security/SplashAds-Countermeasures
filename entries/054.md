### What it does

Leverage Android native VPN/Private DNS to take over network requests and use ad/tracking filtering rules to block matching traffic before the resource is downloaded.

### Limits and notes

Not exclusive to splash ads; first-party/shared domains, cached or packaged ads in apps are more difficult to handle, and local VPN mode may also conflict with other VPNs.

### Before use

Will take over part of the network connection or DNS resolution

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** app's DNS/network requests enter AdGuard's local VPN (VpnService) or Private DNS filtering path.<br>
**How it decides:** Do not identify "ad pages" on the screen, but match the target domain, URL/request characteristics with ad/tracking filtering rules; enable further inspection of partially encrypted traffic when HTTPS filtering is enabled.<br>
**What it does:** Blocks matching DNS resolution or network requests during the inventory load phase.<br>
**Possible result:** The corresponding advertising image, video or configuration cannot be downloaded normally, so some network-loaded ads will not appear.

### Coverage

Both (source network blocking, conditional)｜app advertising/tracking resource requests that can be identified by DNS, URL or HTTPS filtering rules

### Intervention point

Local VPN (VpnService)/Private DNS + Filtering Rules
