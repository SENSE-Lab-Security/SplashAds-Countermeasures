### What it does

Removes Sponsored conversation rows before Gmail inbox ad row layout measurement.

### Limits and notes

Targets Sponsored rows in Gmail inbox only; layout or obfuscated code changes may break targeting.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** Gmail Hide Ads are injected into Gmail via LSPosed/LSPatch and intervene when the inbox conversation line enters the layout process.<br>
**How it decides:** It targets the project's positioned Sponsored session row and its layout path, rather than identifying screenshots.<br>
**What it does:** Removes ad rows before they participate in measurement/layout or remove them from layout.<br>
**Possible result:** Sponsored lines in your Gmail inbox no longer appear properly or take up space.

### Coverage
