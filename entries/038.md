### What it does

Reduce ad intrusion by detecting Spotify ads and controlling play/mute.

### Limits and notes

Only for Spotify audio ads and does not handle mobile app opening ads, sensor triggers, or cross-app jumps.

### Before use

Check the source, version, and permissions before use.

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** The tool observes what is currently playing on Spotify via the media session or target application state.<br>
**How it decides:** When judging, determine whether the current clip is an ad based on the playback/media metadata.<br>
**What it does:** After a hit, use playback controls, mute or skip to reduce ad intrusion.<br>
**Possible result:** As a result, only Spotify audio ads are affected, and mobile app opening ads or cross-app jumps are not processed.

### Coverage

Display-only｜Spotify plays audio ad clips in the stream

### Intervention point

Media session/playback status detection + automatic mute or playback control
