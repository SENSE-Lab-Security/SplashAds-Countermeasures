### What it does

Hide ads and promoted content from your Instagram feed and Stories.

### Limits and notes

Only for feed/Stories ads that Instagram has adapted; it may become invalid after the internal data structure changes.

### Before use

May affect system security or application stability

## Technical notes

### Working method

**Where it starts:** UnclutterIG injected into Instagram,<br>
**How it decides:** Blocks targeted ad/promotion entries when generating content in Feeds and Stories.<br>
**What it does:** It filters out these contents by modifying internal data or View logic,<br>
**Possible result:** No need to find the close button on the screen.

### Coverage

Display-only｜Advertising/promoted content in Instagram feed and Stories
